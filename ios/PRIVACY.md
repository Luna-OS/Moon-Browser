# Privacy Policy for Luma (iPhone and iPad)

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Luma is a web browser made by LunaOS. Its source code is public at
<https://github.com/Luna-OS/Moon-Browser>.

## What Luma collects

Nothing. Luma has no accounts, no sync, no analytics and no advertising. We run no servers that receive data
from the app, and we have no access to what you do in it.

## What stays on your device

Your bookmarks, history, open tabs, downloads, settings and the cookies and site data of the websites you visit
are stored only on your device. You can delete them in Luma's settings at any time, and they are removed when
you delete the app. Private tabs keep no history, and their cookies and site data are discarded when the last
private tab is closed.

## When Luma goes online

- **For the pages you open.** Websites you visit receive what any browser sends, such as your IP address, and
  may set cookies. Their own privacy policies apply.
- **For your searches.** What you type into the address bar as a search is sent to the search engine you chose
  in settings.
- **For website icons.** Luma loads the icon of a site you visit from that site.
- **For its filter lists.** Luma Shield downloads its lists of ads and trackers from GitHub
  (`raw.githubusercontent.com`). GitHub sees your IP address when that happens, as with any download.

## Permissions

Luma asks for the camera, the microphone or your location only when a website you are visiting requests them,
and only passes them on if you agree.

## Children

Luma is a general-purpose browser and is not directed at children.
